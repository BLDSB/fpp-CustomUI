    Alerts

    Send an email alert if a scheduled playlist is not playing within the configured delay. Uses Gmail with an App Password (requires 2-Step Verification on your Google account). For SMS, enter your carrier's email-to-text address as the recipient (e.g. 5551234567@vtext.com for Verizon, @txt.att.net for AT&T). Fill in up to three recipients and every alert goes to all of them.

    Create Pixel Overlay Models

    This writes the pixel overlay models to the controller and restarts it. Zones with an imported layout get their full matrix shape and channel range. Zones without one keep the channel data already set on the controller — nothing you configured there is overwritten.

    A zone that has neither is created as an empty placeholder, and you must set its channel data on the Pixel Overlay Models page before it will work.
