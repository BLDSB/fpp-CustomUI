2. SCOPE OF THE SOFTWARE

2.1 Scenes and Custom Colors

The Software allows licensed facility staff to create and apply scenes, meaning fixed, non-animated color and intensity looks across one or more lighting zones, on installed full-RGB architectural fixtures without requiring third-party programming assistance.
